Most control paradigms for lower-limb exoskeletons are developed under steady-state locomotor conditions. We investigate stability augmentation under unstable postures and external perturbations without prescribing reference joint kinematics. The proposed paradigm defines a center of mass-based stability indicator and employs control barrier functions to enforce user-specific safety constraints. A nonlinear disturbance observer estimates voluntary human input and perturbations for incorporation into the controller. Preliminary experiments with a bilateral hip exoskeleton evaluate the approach during leaning and randomized slip perturbations.
